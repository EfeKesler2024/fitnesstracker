Account and Data Deletion

Updated: 15 September 2026

You can delete your account and your data at any time. There are two ways, and both have the same result.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FitnessTracker and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom
  4. Read the warning and confirm

Your account and data are deleted as soon as you confirm. This action cannot be undone.

If you have not signed in for a long time, the app may ask you to sign in once more before deleting. This is a security measure so that someone who picks up your phone cannot delete your account.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot reach the app — lost phone, app already uninstalled — write to:

fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com

  • Send the request from the email address of your account; this is how we verify your identity
  • Subject line: "Account deletion request"
  • We complete requests within 30 days at the latest and confirm when done

What gets deleted

DataWhereWhat happens
Email address, name, password, account identifier Firebase (Google servers) Deleted
Profile: age, height, weight, gender, goals Your phone Deleted
Meal, water and workout records Your phone Deleted
Firebase connection and security logs Google servers May remain for Google's own retention periods

After deletion no personal data of yours remains in our hands. You may register again with the same email address, but your old records will not return.

I only want to delete my data, not my account

You can delete individual meal and workout records inside the app. To clear everything at once without touching your account, use your phone's Settings → Apps → FitnessTracker → Storage → Clear data.
