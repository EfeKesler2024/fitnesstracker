Account and Data Deletion

Updated: 30 September 2026

You can delete your account and your data at any time. There are two ways, and both have the same result.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FitnessTracker and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom
  4. Read the warning and confirm

As soon as you confirm, your account, your data in the cloud and the data on this phone are deleted. This action cannot be undone.

If you have an AI Coach subscription: Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription, and you will keep being charged. Cancel it separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

If you have not signed in within the last few minutes, the app asks you to sign in once more before deleting. This is a security measure, so that your account cannot be deleted if someone else gets hold of your phone. Deletion also needs an internet connection: so that your account is never deleted while your cloud data remains, the process does not start without one, and you can try again once you are back online.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app (you lost your phone, uninstalled the app, and so on), write to:

fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com

  • Send the request from your account's email address; this is how we verify your identity
  • Writing "Account deletion request" in the subject line is enough
  • We will complete your request and let you know within 30 days at the latest

What is deleted

DataWhereWhat happens
Your email address, name, password, account ID Firebase Authentication (Google servers) Deleted
Your profile (age, height, weight, sex, goals), meals, saved meals, workouts, water, step and weight logs, saved programs Firebase Firestore (Google servers) and your phone Deleted
Your AI Coach chat history and AI Coach consent choices Your phone only Deleted
Your AI Coach subscription link and remaining-questions indicator Firebase (Google servers) Deleted
Irreversible digest of the purchase token and that day's question count (no name or email) Firebase (Google servers) Deleted automatically after 48 hours. This prevents the same day's question allowance from being reset by deleting and recreating an account
Questions and image-analysis photos you previously sent to AI Coach OpenAI Never stored on our server. OpenAI's own retention periods apply on its side
Your Google Play purchase and payment history Google Play Stays in your Google account; subject to Google's rules
Connection and security records kept by Firebase Google servers May remain for Google's own retention periods

After deletion, apart from the digest kept for 48 hours as described above, no personal data about you remains with us. You can sign up again with the same email address, but your old records will not come back. If your subscription is still valid, you can link it to your new account with Restore purchases.

I only want to delete my data, not my account

You can delete meals, workouts and saved meals one by one in the app; a deleted record is also removed from your other phones. If you want all of your data deleted but your account kept, write to fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com from your account's email address; we will complete your request within 30 days at the latest.

Your phone's Settings → Apps → FitnessTracker → Storage → Clear data only deletes the copy on this phone. Because your data is kept in the cloud, it comes back when you sign in again.
