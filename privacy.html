FitnessTracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

This page explains what information FitnessTracker collects, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. We have tried to keep it short and to describe what the app actually does.

In short: you need an account to use the app, and only account information (your email address and account identifier) is stored on Google's servers. Your fitness data — weight, meals, workouts, water intake — stays on your phone only and is never sent to us or anyone else. No ads, no analytics, no data selling.

1. Who is responsible

  • App: FitnessTracker (efekesler.fitness)
  • Contact: fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com

2. What we process

Stored on Google's servers (Firebase Authentication)

We use Google's Firebase Authentication to create your account and verify your sign-in. It stores:

DataWhy
Your email addressTo identify your account, let you sign in, and send password reset emails
Your password (irreversibly hashed)To verify your sign-in. The plain password is never stored and nobody, including the developer, can read it
Your nameTo address you by name in the app
Account identifier (uid)To know which account the data on your phone belongs to
Account creation and last sign-in timeRecorded automatically by Firebase
Connection data such as IP addressRecorded automatically by Firebase for security and abuse prevention

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google tells us only your name and email address. We never see your Google password and we do not access anything else in your Google account — not your contacts, files or calendar.

Stored on your phone only

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your device. None of it is sent over the internet and we have no access to it:

  • Gender, age, height, weight
  • Your activity level and goal type (lose, maintain, gain)
  • Your daily calorie, step and water goals
  • Meals you log (calories and an optional note)
  • Workouts you record (type, duration, intensity, calories)
  • Your daily water and step records

This has an important consequence: your fitness data is not backed up. If you lose your phone, uninstall the app or move to a new device, you can sign in with your account but your history will not come back. We plan to change this in the future and will update this page when we do.

3. What we do not collect

The app never requests, reads or collects:

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, messages, photos or files
  • Other apps on your phone
  • Camera and microphone
  • Health sensors such as heart rate

The only permission the app requests is internet access, and it is used solely for sign-up and sign-in.

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or similar). Your usage is not tracked.

4. Why we process this data

  • Performance of a contract: the app does not work without an account, so email and password are required.
  • Legitimate interest: Firebase's connection logs exist to prevent abuse of accounts.
  • Consent: you enter your fitness details yourself and they stay on your phone. You may also skip profile setup entirely.

5. Who we share with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. Your account data is held by a single provider:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — the authentication service. Google processes this data on its own servers. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Apart from this, we share nothing with anyone unless legally required to do so, for example by a court order.

Firebase servers may be located outside your country. By creating an account you accept this transfer of your account data.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account, at which point it is removed from Firebase.
  • Fitness data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it from your phone completely.
  • Firebase security logs: Google's own retention periods apply.

7. Deleting your account and data

  1. In the app: Profile tab → Delete my account. Once confirmed, your account is removed from Firebase and your data from your phone immediately.
  2. By email: send a deletion request to fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com from the email address of your account. We complete requests within 30 days at the latest.

See the account deletion page for details.

8. Security

  • Your password is stored by Google in an irreversibly hashed form; the plain password does not exist anywhere.
  • All traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data on your phone lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

No system is perfectly secure. We recommend choosing a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

9. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, and profile setup does not accept an age below 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to or restrict its processing, and to receive a copy of it. Users in Türkiye have these rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

To exercise them, write to fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days at the latest. Most of your data is already on your phone and under your direct control, and you can delete your account yourself from inside the app.

11. Changes to this policy

This page may change as the app changes. For significant changes we update the effective date and notify you inside the app. A change such as moving data to the cloud would be stated explicitly.

12. Contact

Questions: fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com
