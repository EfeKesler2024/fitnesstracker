FitnessTracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This page explains what information FitnessTracker collects, where it is stored, who it is shared with and how you can delete it. We have tried to keep it short and to describe what the app actually does.

Summary: You need an account to use the app. Your account and the fitness data you enter (your profile, meals, workouts, water, step and weight logs) are stored on Google Firebase servers, linked to your account, so your records are not lost when you change phones. Questions you ask AI Coach and that day's conversation are sent to OpenAI to generate an answer; if you agree, your profile details (age, height, weight, sex, activity level, goal, daily targets) are added so answers fit you. Your name, email address and meal/workout logs are not sent, and questions and answers are not stored on our servers. No ads, no analytics, no selling of data.

2. What data we process

Account information (Firebase Authentication)

We use Google's Firebase Authentication service to create your account and verify your sign-in. It stores:

DataWhy
Your email addressTo identify your account, let you sign in, and send verification and password reset emails
Your password (irreversibly hashed)To verify your sign-in. The plain password is never stored; nobody, including the developer, can see it
Your nameTo address you by name in the app
Account ID (uid)To know which account your data belongs to
Account creation and last sign-in timeRecorded automatically by Firebase
Connection information (such as IP address)Recorded automatically by Firebase for security and abuse prevention

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google tells us only your name and email address. We do not see your Google password and do not access anything else in your Google account (contacts, files, calendar).

Your fitness data (Firebase Firestore)

The information you enter is stored both on your phone and in Google's Firebase Firestore database, in an area that only your account can access. This lets your records follow you when you sign in on another phone and keeps them safe if you lose your phone. What is stored:

  • Profile: your name, email address, age, height, weight, sex, activity level and goal type (lose, maintain, gain)
  • Goals: your daily calorie, step and water goals
  • Meals: calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, meal name and your optional note
  • Saved meals: meal templates you define yourself
  • Workouts: type, duration, intensity and calories
  • Daily logs: your step count and water intake
  • Weight logs (with dates)
  • Workout programs you save (programs prepared by AI Coach that you chose to save)

This data may count as health-related information. We use it only to provide the app's features to you; we do not use it for advertising, profiling or sale, and we do not share it with anyone else. Firestore security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data.

Step counter

To count your steps, the app uses your phone's step counter sensor, and Android asks you for the "Physical activity" permission. So that steps are counted while the app is closed, the sensor is read in the background roughly every 15 minutes. Only your daily step total is stored; your location, route or type of movement is not recorded. If you do not grant the permission, the app keeps working; steps are simply not counted.

AI Coach

AI Coach is offered with a monthly subscription and using it is optional. If you use it:

  • "Ask AI Coach": The text of your question, the app language and, so it can follow the conversation, your earlier questions and answers from the same day are sent through our server (Google Cloud Functions) to OpenAI to generate an answer. Conversations from previous days are not sent.
    Profile details (only with your consent): You are asked at your first question. If you agree, your profile's age, height, weight, sex, activity level, goal and daily calorie/step/water targets are sent with the question so answers fit you. If you decline, answers are general and you are not asked again that day. Profile details of users under 18 are never sent.
    Your name, email address, account ID and your meal, workout, water, step and weight logs are not included. Your questions, profile details and the answers are not stored on our server. Your chat history is kept only on your phone, is excluded from phone backups, and is deleted when you delete your account.
    If you write health information into your question yourself (for example an illness, medication or injury), that information is sent to OpenAI together with the question. We recommend not including anything that identifies you, such as your name or ID number.
  • Program builder: Currently runs on your phone using fixed rules and sends nothing out. If it is later connected to an AI service, your age, height, weight and sex will be sent without your name or email address to tailor the program, you will be asked for separate consent, and we will update this policy.
  • Image analysis: Currently shows only a sample result. The photo you pick from your gallery never leaves your phone, is not uploaded to any server and is not stored. The app does not ask for general access to your gallery; it only sees the single photo you pick.

Subscription and payment (Google Play)

You buy the AI Coach subscription through Google Play. Your card details and the payment are handled entirely by Google; we do not see or store your payment information. To check whether your subscription is valid:

  • The purchase token issued by Google Play is stored on our server, linked to your account, and before each AI Coach question we ask Google Play whether the subscription is still active. The app itself cannot read this token.
  • To count your daily question allowance (10 questions a day), an irreversible digest (hash) of the purchase token and that day's question count are stored.

3. What we do not collect

The app does not request, read or collect:

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, messages or files
  • Other apps on your phone
  • Camera or microphone
  • Other health sensors such as heart rate (only the step counter is used)
  • Your card or bank details

Permissions the app requests: internet access (for sign-in, syncing your data and AI Coach) and physical activity (for the step counter).

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or similar). Your usage habits are not tracked.

4. Why we process data

  • Performance of a contract: The app does not work without an account, so email and password are required. Verifying your subscription and counting your daily allowance are needed to provide the service you paid for.
  • Legitimate interest: The connection records kept by Firebase are for preventing account abuse. Keeping the purchase-token digest for 48 hours after account deletion prevents the same day's question allowance from being reset by deleting and recreating an account.
  • Explicit consent: You enter your fitness and health-related information (weight, meals, workouts, steps) yourself, and syncing it to the cloud is based on this policy, which you accept when creating an account. You may leave it out; the app lets you skip profile setup. Using AI Coach and sending it questions is entirely your choice. Sending your profile details to OpenAI for AI Coach is based on the separate explicit consent you give in the app.

5. Who it is shared with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use only the service providers needed to run the app:

ProviderPurposeWhich data
Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions) Account, storing and syncing your data, AI Coach server Account information, your fitness data, subscription records
Google Play Selling the subscription, payment and subscription verification Purchase information (Google takes the payment)

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · OpenAI's privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. The app asks OpenAI not to store questions on its side; under its own policy OpenAI may keep this data for a limited time to prevent abuse, and states that it does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default.

Apart from this, we do not share any information with anyone unless required by law (for example a court order).

International transfers

Google's and OpenAI's servers may be located outside Türkiye, including in the United States. By creating an account and using AI Coach, you give explicit consent to the data listed above being transferred abroad to provide these services. If you do not use AI Coach, no data is sent to OpenAI.

6. How long it is kept

  • Your account information and fitness data in the cloud: Until you delete your account.
  • Data on your phone and your AI Coach chat history: Until you delete it, delete your account or uninstall the app.
  • AI Coach questions and profile details sent with them: Not stored on our server. OpenAI's own retention periods apply on its side.
  • Subscription record: For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account the subscription link is deleted immediately; the purchase-token digest and that day's question count are deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • Firebase security records: Google's own retention periods apply.

7. Deleting your account and data

There are two ways:

  1. In the app: Profile tab → Delete my account. Once you confirm, your account, your data in the cloud and your data on the phone are deleted.
  2. By email: Send a deletion request to fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com from your account's email address. We will complete your request within 30 days at the latest.

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. You need to cancel it separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. See the account deletion page for details.

8. Security

  • Your password is stored irreversibly hashed by Google; the plain password is never kept anywhere.
  • All communication between the app, Firebase and OpenAI is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your data in the cloud can be accessed only with your own account; one user cannot read another user's data.
  • The OpenAI access key is kept only on the server; it is not inside the app.
  • Data on your phone is kept in the private area Android reserves for the app; other apps cannot read it.

Still, no system is completely secure. To protect your account, we recommend a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

9. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13, and an age under 13 cannot be entered during profile setup. The AI Coach program feature is for ages 18 and over, and profile details of users under 18 are not sent to AI Coach. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Under Article 11 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, to request its deletion or destruction, and to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis. You can withdraw your explicit consent at any time: to withdraw the profile consent you gave for AI Coach, email us or delete your account.

To use these rights, simply write to fitness.tracker.2027@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days at the latest.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the app changes. If there is a significant change, we will update the effective date and notify you in the app. In particular, if a new type of data starts being sent to AI Coach, we will say so clearly.
