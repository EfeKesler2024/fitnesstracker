6. AI Coach and subscription

What you get

  • The app's core features (tracking, statistics, exercise library, ready-made programs) are free.
  • "Ask AI Coach" requires a monthly subscription. A subscription bought with a Google account is linked to one FitnessTracker account and works on every phone where you sign in to that account. The same purchase cannot be used by another FitnessTracker account at the same time.
  • The subscription lets you ask up to 10 questions a day. The allowance resets every day at midnight Türkiye time; unused questions do not carry over. If a question cannot be answered, that question is given back.
  • Image analysis is also included: you can have up to 5 photos analyzed a day. This allowance is counted separately from questions, resets every day at midnight Türkiye time, and a failed analysis is given back.
  • The program builder is also included: you can have up to 3 weekly programs created a day (a new version with Regenerate counts as one). This allowance is counted separately, resets every day at midnight Türkiye time, and a program that could not be created is given back.
