6. Premium membership and AI Coach

Free and Premium features

  • Free: account and profile; daily calorie, meal, macronutrient, water and step tracking; the food database and saved meals; workout logging and history; the exercise library.
  • Requires a Premium membership (monthly subscription): Statistics (weight, calorie, macronutrient, water and step charts, and logging weight from that screen), the ready-made and saved training programs in the Workouts section, and the three AI Coach features: asking questions, image analysis and the program builder.
  • When the membership ends, access to these sections closes; your previously entered records are not deleted because of this.
  • A Premium subscription bought with a Google account is linked to one FitnessTracker account and works on every phone where you sign in to that account. The same purchase cannot be used by another FitnessTracker account at the same time.
  • The subscription lets you ask up to 3 questions a day. The allowance resets every day at midnight Türkiye time; unused questions do not carry over.
  • Image analysis is also included: you can have up to 2 photos analyzed a day. This allowance is counted separately from questions and resets every day at midnight Türkiye time.
  • The program builder is also included: you can have up to 1 weekly program created per week (a new version with Regenerate counts as one). This allowance is counted separately, and resets on Monday at 00:00 Türkiye time. Unused program allowance does not carry over to the next week.

If you cut the connection while a request is running (for example by turning off the internet or fully closing the app), the allowance is used.
